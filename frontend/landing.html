Your brain is not a roadmap.

Founders don't think in checklists.

You jump from product to hiring to funding to a customer call to legal to another product idea, to something you suddenly remembered three weeks ago.

  • Product
  • Hiring
  • Funding
  • Customer
  • Legal
  • Another idea
  • That thing from 3 weeks ago

FounderOS keeps all of those thoughts visible, without forcing them into a rigid sequence.

Dump. Map. Explore. Decide what deserves attention.

  1. 01

    Dump

    Write naturally.

    “I want to launch the sensor product, but I'm not sure we can afford another engineer. We also need compliance approval and I don't know whether we should raise money first.”
  2. 02

    Map

    FounderOS pulls out Product, Engineering, Hiring, Money, Compliance, Funding, risks, decisions and unknowns, then connects them. It merges into your existing map; it never starts over.

  3. 03

    Explore

    Click any node. Ask about it. See its relationships, dependencies, assumptions and the questions nobody has answered yet. Switch lenses to see only money, risk or evidence.

  4. 04

    Decide

    FounderOS doesn't decide for you. AI suggestions are labelled as proposals, and nothing becomes a fact on your map until you accept it.

Your startup isn't a checklist. It's a network.

Your product, people, money, customers, risks, decisions, experiments and weird 2 AM ideas all influence each other. FounderOS makes that network visible, so you can see which decision touches seven others, which assumption nothing has tested, and which idea has no customer.

  • What dominates my attention?
  • What is isolated?
  • What depends on what?
  • What have I actually validated?
  • What am I not seeing?
